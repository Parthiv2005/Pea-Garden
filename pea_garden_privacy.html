Pea Garden: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Pea Garden does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.
  • The game does not use the internet. The only permission it uses is vibration, for light haptic taps (it can be turned off in Settings).
  • Your progress is saved in a file on your own device. If Android Auto Backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include that file in your personal Google backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings; the developer never receives it.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes the save file from the device.

Children: Pea Garden is suitable for all ages, including children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and contains no ads, no third-party services and no links out of the game.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Questions: contact the developer at the email address shown on the game's Google Play page.
